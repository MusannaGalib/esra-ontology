# ESRA ontology

The terms of the ESRA namespace, `https://data.energystoragera.org/ns#`, used by the ESRA data platform of the
Energy Storage Research Alliance: the classes, properties and links of the working graph, the vocabulary of the
semantic layer, and the terms a person reviewed and approved.

- **File:** [`esra.ttl`](esra.ttl) (Turtle, OWL). 129 terms in version 2026-10-29.
- **Live:** `https://data.energystoragera.org/ns` (Turtle, JSON-LD or a readable page, by content negotiation),
  once the platform version that serves it is deployed.
- **Versions:** each release is a git tag `v<date>`, matching the file's `owl:versionInfo`.
- **Source:** generated by `Data_pipeline/ontology/build_esra_ttl.py` in the ESRA platform from its ontology files;
  do not edit `esra.ttl` by hand.
- **Links to other ontologies:** where a term has a match in EMMO or BattINFO, it is linked with `rdfs:subClassOf`
  or `skos:closeMatch`.
- **Gaps:** 52 terms carry a label but no definition yet; they are listed in the file's header comment and will be
  defined in later versions.
